## About Me

My name is Konstantin Starodubov. I live in Moscow and hold a Ph.D. in Technical Sciences.

I specialize in information security and identification technologies. I've published research on biometrics and presented talks on Open API security.

### Professional Experience

I worked in the Information Security Department at the Bank of Russia. Currently, I lead Information Security Compliance in Yandex's fintech division.

I'm interested in building information security requirements that:
- genuinely improve the security of products and infrastructure;
- don't slow down development and business growth;
- work in practice, not just on paper.

### What This Blog Is About

I write about:
- information security in fintech;
- security compliance: implementing standards and verifying their effectiveness;
- practical approaches to protecting products and systems;
- balancing security and usability.

Occasionally, I'll share notes about photography—my main hobby outside of work.
